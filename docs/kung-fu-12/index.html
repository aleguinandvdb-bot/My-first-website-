Welcome to Kung Fu 12

Specializing in authentic Szechuan cuisine, Kung Fu 12 brings traditional Chinese flavors to Rockville. Every dish is prepared fresh to order using high-quality ingredients and time-honored recipes. Whether you're craving spicy Szechuan heat or classic comfort dishes, you'll find it here.

Signature Dishes

Mapo Tofu

Silky tofu in a spicy, aromatic sauce. Numbing Szechuan peppercorns, garlic, ginger. A classic done right.

Chongqing Chicken

Crispy chicken tossed with dried chilies, peppercorns, and aromatics. Bold, intense flavors.

Kung Pao Chicken

Tender chicken, peanuts, peppers, and scallions in a savory-sweet sauce with a spicy kick.

Dan Dan Noodles

Chewy noodles in a rich, spicy sesame sauce with ground pork, pickled vegetables, and peanuts.

Hot Pot

Interactive dining. Simmer fresh meats, seafood, and vegetables in your own broth. Build your experience.

Fried Rice

Fluffy rice with egg, protein, and vegetables. Choose your protein. Fast and satisfying.
