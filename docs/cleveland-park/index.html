About the shop

Wine, spirits, and beer — chosen, not just stocked.

Cleveland Park Fine Wines & Spirits is a neighborhood shop on Connecticut Avenue NW, a short walk from the Cleveland Park Metro. The shelves run deep rather than wide — a working selection built for people who actually cook and entertain nearby, not a warehouse of everything.

Staff behind the counter know the shelves by heart. Ask what pairs with dinner, what's new in from a small importer, or what to bring to a friend's — that's the kind of shop this is.
