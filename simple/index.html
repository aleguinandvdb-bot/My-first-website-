Our Story

A corner of Provence in downtown Rockville

Chateau de Rockville opened as a small corner of Provence in downtown Rockville — a place for stone-milled pastry, patient espresso, and mornings that don't feel rushed.

Everything on the counter is baked, brewed, or built in-house, in small batches, every day.

We wanted a café that felt like a doorway to somewhere slower.

— The founders, Chateau de Rockville
